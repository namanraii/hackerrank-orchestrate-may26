# How to update Claude for Android

If you aren’t seeing the latest features on Claude for Android, you may need to
update your mobile app manually by following these instructions from Google
:
Open the Google Play Store app .
At the top right, tap the profile icon.
Tap “Manage apps & device.”
Under
Updates available
, tap “See details.”
Tap “Update” next to Claude for Android.
