# Where can I go for Claude API support and assistance?

For our API users, we currently maintain three main resources:
Our
Client Software Development Kits (SDKs)
;
Our
Claude Docs
; and
Our community
Discord
where developers can share ideas and exchange tips.
If you need to
contact our Support team
, you can do so by clicking on the message icon in the bottom right of our Help Center, or by clicking your initials in the lower left corner of your Claude Console account, then "Get help."
