# I’m encountering 429 errors, and I’m worried my rate limit is too low. What should I do?

Your Claude API organization's rate limit depends on your usage tier, which will increase automatically from Tier 1 to Tier 4 as you reach certain thresholds while using the API. Your usage tier also determines your monthly spend limit, which increases as you progress through the tiers. You can see your organization’s tier at
Settings > Limits
.
Refer to the
Rate limits page in our Claude docs
for more information.
