# How to use Claude in your preferred language

Claude is available in multiple languages on web and desktop applications.
Supported Languages
English
French
German
Hindi
Indonesian
Italian
Japanese
Korean
Portuguese (Brazilian)
Spanish (Latin America)
Spanish (Spain)
Changing your language setting
On Web and Desktop
Click your profile icon in the lower left corner.
Go to the "Language" section.
Choose your preferred language.
The interface will update automatically.
Note:
Even if you've changed your language settings, you can still chat with Claude in any language. Claude will converse with you in the language you use.
